# Teammate setup

Follow [SETUP_AND_DEMO.md](SETUP_AND_DEMO.md), which is the current required deployment guide.

The roles are fixed:

- Laptop A runs the NameNode, ResourceManager, DataNode 1, and NodeManager 1.
- Laptop B runs DataNode 2 and NodeManager 2.
- Both workers are on separate physical laptops and connect to the same HDFS/YARN master.
